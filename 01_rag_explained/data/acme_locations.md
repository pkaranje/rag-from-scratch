# Locations

Acme Robotics is headquartered at 4410 Pearl Street, Boulder, Colorado. The building was
formerly a bicycle repair warehouse, which is why the loading dock is unusually narrow —
a fact the operations team frequently complains about.

The Porto, Portugal office opened in 2034 and has 11 employees, all in customer support
and localization. There is no manufacturing at the Porto office; all robots are assembled
in Boulder and shipped internationally.
