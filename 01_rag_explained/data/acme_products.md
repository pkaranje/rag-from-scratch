# Products

Acme Robotics' flagship product is the **Junebug**, a knee-high warehouse robot that
carries shelving pods to human pickers. The Junebug ships with a 14-hour battery and a
top speed of 1.8 meters per second.

The second product, released a year after the Junebug, is the **Cricket**, a smaller
robot designed for narrow-aisle pharmacy warehouses. The Cricket cannot carry pods —
instead it carries individual bins up to 9 kilograms.

Both robots run on Acme's in-house fleet-coordination software, internally called
**HiveMind**, which is not sold separately.
