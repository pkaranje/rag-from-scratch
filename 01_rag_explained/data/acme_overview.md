# Acme Robotics

Acme Robotics was founded in 2031 by Priya Nandan and Tobias Reyes in a rented garage in
Boulder, Colorado. The company builds autonomous warehouse robots for mid-sized logistics
companies that cannot afford enterprise robotics contracts.

As of the last shareholder letter, Acme Robotics employs 214 people across two offices:
the Boulder headquarters and a smaller satellite office in Porto, Portugal, which handles
European customer support.

The company's mission statement, printed on the wall of the Boulder lobby, reads:
"Move the boxes so people can do the interesting work."
