# Founders

**Priya Nandan** was previously a mechanical engineer at a drone delivery startup. She
designed the Junebug's wheel suspension system and serves as CEO.

**Tobias Reyes** came from a background in warehouse operations, not engineering. He spent
six years managing a distribution center before co-founding Acme Robotics, and today
serves as COO. His frustration with existing warehouse robots — which he described as
"built by people who have never worked a night shift" — was the original inspiration for
the company.

Acme Robotics has never taken venture capital funding. It was bootstrapped entirely from a
$180,000 loan against Tobias's house.
