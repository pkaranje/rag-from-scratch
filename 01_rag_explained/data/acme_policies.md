# Return & Support Policy

Acme Robotics offers a 45-day return window on the Junebug and Cricket robots, longer
than the industry-standard 30 days, because Tobias Reyes insisted customers "should get a
full billing cycle to know if it actually helps."

Support tickets are answered within 4 business hours during North American daytime hours,
and within 8 business hours for tickets filed through the Porto office.

Robots are covered by a 2-year hardware warranty. The HiveMind fleet software receives
free updates for the lifetime of the hardware.
